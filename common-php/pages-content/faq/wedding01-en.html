Frequently Asked Questions about Live Saxophone for Weddings & Events

As a professional musician performing live across concert stages and private events, weddings are among my most requested bookings. Here are the answers to the most common questions about hiring me for your special day.

Performance Formats & Schedule

Which parts of the wedding can I perform live saxophone for?
Through SAXONARA, I provide live music tailored to every key moment of your wedding:
  • Ceremony: Elegant acoustic or amplified music for walking down the aisle, readings, rings exchange, and exit.
  • Cocktail Hour / Drinks Reception: Solo Sax or Sax & DJ set featuring Pop, Chillout, Deep House, and Jazz vibes.
  • Dinner / Grand Entrances: High-energy live moments (entrance of the newlyweds, cake cutting).
  • Party / After-Party: High-octane Sax + DJ live show with wireless LED setup performing dance and club hits to keep the dance floor packed.
What music formats do I offer for your event?
I offer several versatile options to match your style:
  • Solo Sax: Ideal for ceremonies, cocktail hours, and welcome drinks.
  • Sax & DJ Live Show: The ultimate party performance for the evening reception, blending live saxophone energy with club DJ tracks.
  • Duo / Ensemble (Violin or Percussion): Enhance the atmosphere by pairing saxophone with elegant live violin or dynamic live percussions for your ceremony or party.
Can you book me for just the cocktail hour or evening party?
Yes, absolutely. You can hire me for specific moments (e.g., just the ceremony, cocktail hour, or party) or choose full-day music packages covering your entire wedding timeline from ceremony to late-night party.
What style of music and repertoire do I perform?
My repertoire is highly versatile: Pop and Rock covers on saxophone, Chillout, Deep House, Funk, Jazz, Bossa Nova, and current electronic club hits for the main party.
Can you request custom songs for key moments?
Yes! You can choose special songs for key moments like walking down the aisle, the entrance to the reception, or your first dance. I work closely with couples ahead of time to tailor the setlist to your musical preferences.

Logistics, AV Equipment & Sound

What sound equipment is provided with the performance?
To ensure optimal sound quality across outdoor gardens, villas, or indoor halls, high-end professional sound and lighting equipment is required.
When full AV equipment is required, I partner directly with specialized production partners such as SoundEvent. This allows us to supply and integrate top-tier sound and light setups seamlessly.
What technical requirements are needed at the venue?
If your venue or DJ already provides the sound system, I will send over my technical rider specifying the input connections for saxophone and backing tracks. If we supply the sound equipment through SoundEvent, we handle setup and soundcheck end-to-end.
In all cases, all that is needed on-site is a standard power outlet (220V) near the performance area and a flat space protected from rain or direct sunlight.
Do I move around during the live performance?
Absolutely. I perform using a professional wireless microphone system attached to my saxophone, allowing me to roam freely among your guests, join you on the dance floor, or move effortlessly between ceremony and reception areas.
What happens if performance locations change during the wedding?
We plan all multi-location transitions in advance so the music flows naturally between ceremony, drinks reception, and party areas. SAXONARA can coordinate directly with your venue manager or wedding planner to handle all multi-stage setups seamlessly.
How early do I arrive at the venue?
I typically arrive 60 to 90 minutes before performance start time to set up, conduct soundchecks, and coordinate with your wedding coordinator.
