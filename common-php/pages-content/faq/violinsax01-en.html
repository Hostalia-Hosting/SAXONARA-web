🎷🎻 About the Duo & Musical Style

What makes the combination of Violin and Saxophone so special?
The fusion of the elegance and agility of the violin with the warmth and high-energy presence of the saxophone creates an extraordinary sonic contrast. This combination allows us to seamlessly perform everything from emotional classical pieces to modern, high-energy arrangements of Pop, Deep House, Chill-out, and Dance Hits, delivering a much richer visual and auditory experience than a solo instrument.
What kind of music can you play?
Our repertoire is completely versatile and adapts perfectly to every moment of your event:
  • Ceremonies & Special Moments: Emotional movie soundtracks, adapted classics, Pop ballads, and customized entry songs.
  • Cocktail Hour & Drinks Receptions: Elegant sessions of Lounge, Smooth Jazz, Ibiza Deep House, and modern international Pop covers.
  • Party & DJ Shows: High-energy live sessions combining freestyle improvisation over House, Dance, and timeless party anthems.
Can we request special songs that are not on your song list?
Absolutely! We love personalizing the soundtrack to your big day. For key moments (such as the ceremony entrance, first dance, or a special party moment), we can arrange and coordinate exclusive custom versions for Violin & Sax. Just let us know in advance!

Can you move around while performing?
Yes! We use high-end wireless microphone systems, allowing us to move freely among your guests, interact with the crowd, and adapt effortlessly to any garden, terrace, or indoor venue layout.

🔊 Technical Requirements & Logistics

Do we need to worry about renting sound equipment?
No, we take care of everything. Our quotes include a professional, self-contained sound system and wireless microphones tailored to your venue and guest count. If your venue already has its own AV setup or sound limits, we coordinate directly with their technical team.
What are your space and power requirements?
  • Space: A minimum area of approximately 2x2 meters for our main setup.
  • Power: A standard single power outlet (220V) nearby to connect our sound gear.
  • Outdoors: For outdoor performances, we require a covered or shaded area to protect our wooden and brass instruments from direct sunlight or rain.
How long does setup and soundcheck take?
We typically arrive 60 to 90 minutes before our performance time to set up, run a discrete soundcheck, and change into performance attire without disrupting your guests.

How far in advance should we book?
For peak season dates (May through October), we recommend booking 6 to 12 months in advance, as weekend dates fill up quickly. However, feel free to reach out to check availability for short-notice dates!
Do you travel for destination weddings and international events?
Yes! We perform regularly across the Costa Brava, Barcelona, the Balearic Islands (Ibiza & Mallorca), across Spain, and for international destination weddings throughout Europe. All travel fees and logistics are clearly detailed in your custom proposal.
