Lake Como Sessions: The Classics: Our New Two-Track Album is Here

Arnald SAXONARA Monday, 16 February 2026

We are thrilled to announce the official release of our latest project: a two-track journey through late-night creativity and reimagined classics. The "Lake Como Sessions: The Classics" mini-album captures the essence of our home studio sessions—blending the warmth of analog instruments with the dusty textures of modern Lo-fi.

Track 1: "All Of Me" (Lo-fi Lake Como Edit)

For this track, we took the iconic jazz standard and stripped it down to its soulful core.

  • The Vibe: Imagine a rainy afternoon, a warm cup of coffee, and the smell of old vinyl.

  • The Gear: You’ll hear the Harley Benton Nashville Nylon providing a mellow rhythmic bed, while the Tenor Saxophone takes the lead with a breathy, intimate performance recorded through the Rode NT2-A.

  • The Magic: Everything was layered using the Boss RC-600, creating a natural, evolving soundscape that honors the original melody while finding a new home in the chillhop world.

Track 2: "Stand By Me" (Lakeside Lofi Version)

The second track is a deep dive into pure experimentation. This is where the multi-instrumentalist spirit really shines.

  • The Concept: A journey through textures. We started with a simple bass groove on the Harley Benton JB-62, added some ethereal keys from the AKAI MPK mini, and let the sax wander through a forest of reverb and delay.

  • The Learning Curve: This track represents our "personal challenge"—learning how to make different instruments "speak" to each other in real-time. It’s raw, organic, and 100% improvised.

Why Two Tracks?

We wanted to show the two sides of our musical identity: the respect for the Jazz tradition and the hunger for Electronic innovation. By pairing a known standard with an original soundscape, we’re inviting you to see how the past and the future of the saxophone can live together on the same playlist.

Support the Journey

This album is the result of countless hours of "trial and error" in the studio. You can now stream it on all major platforms:

  • Spotify: https://open.spotify.com/album/32TLSH8M3ifKI1pOhvmvVT
  • YouTube: https://open.spotify.com/album/32TLSH8M3ifKI1pOhvmvVT

Grab your headphones, hit play, and let the soundscapes take over. Thank you for being part of this independent music journey!
